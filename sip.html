Portfolio Simulator

Free, no-login tool to backtest Indian investments on real historical data. Compare portfolios of mutual funds, NSE indices (Nifty 50, Nifty Next 50 and over 100 more), stocks and ETFs, PPF, EPF, fixed-return assets and inflation, using rolling returns (XIRR) and volatility.

  • Lumpsum simulator: rolling returns of a one-time investment, for one or more portfolios.
  • SIP simulator: rolling XIRR of a monthly SIP, with optional rebalancing and annual step-up.
  • Historical values: NAV or price history of any asset, compared on one chart.

Link straight to a simulation

Every simulation is defined by its URL, so a link reproduces it exactly. Examples:

  • 5-year rolling SIP returns: Nifty 50 vs Parag Parikh Flexi Cap Fund
  • 10-year rolling lumpsum returns: Nifty 50 vs a 60/40 Nifty 50 and PPF mix
  • Nifty 50 against Indian inflation

The URL format is documented in llms.txt. Source code: github.com/asrajavel/portfolio-simulator.
